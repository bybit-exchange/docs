## Overview

Event Contract is a structured product offered by Bybit that allows users to trade on the outcome of market events with a defined payout structure.

## Access

:::caution
Only **market makers** are permitted to call the Event Contract OpenAPI endpoints. To apply for market maker access, please contact your **Relation Manager**.
Public access may be considered in the future.
:::

## WebSocket streams

- Public: [Orderbook](./websocket/public/orderbook) and [Trade](./websocket/public/public-trade).
- Private: [Position](./websocket/private/position), [Execution](./websocket/private/execution), and [Order](./websocket/private/order).

For account transaction records, see [Get Transaction Log](../account/transaction-log).
